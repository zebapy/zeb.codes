Developed and maintained the website for Homes First Vermont, a nonprofit organization focused on housing initiatives in Vermont.

## Project Overview

The project involved creating a modern, accessible website to help the organization reach more people in need of housing assistance and support.

## Key Features

- Responsive design for all devices
- Accessible navigation and content
- Resource information for community members
- Contact and support information
